# Hypothesis Ranking

## Ranked Before Phase 4 Experiments
1. Champion: `four_vertex_lift`
2. Backup: `pair_slack_repair`
3. Reserve: `composite_orbit_templates`

## Why This Was A Defensible Phase 3 Ranking
- `four_vertex_lift` was the only lane that clearly changed the search object instead of repackaging the exact witness bank or the prime-power family.
- `pair_slack_repair` was weaker scientifically, but fast to falsify and directly aligned with the verifier.
- `composite_orbit_templates` overlapped too heavily with cyclic/block-circulant work to justify early budget without stronger structural evidence.

## Novelty Check
- Against the four named string-graph watchlist papers: overlap is superficial retrieval drift only.
- Against Wesley and other book-Ramsey sources: the implemented root artifact has no scientific novelty beyond packaging hygiene.
- The strongest initial novelty candidate was the fixed-old-graph lift.

## Post-Experiment Note
Phase 4 killed the champion lane in its current fixed-old-graph form because both known-step recovery attempts were infeasible. That does not invalidate the Phase 3 ranking as a budget decision; it changes the post-experiment decision.
